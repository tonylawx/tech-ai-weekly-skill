# High-Signal Source Whitelist

Primary information source directory for curating Sunday's Tech & AI Weekly Digest.

---

## 1. Frontier Research Labs & Primary Engineering Releases
- **OpenAI Blog & Research**: Release notes, system cards, research papers, API changelogs.
- **Anthropic Research**: Model cards, prompt engineering updates, red-teaming and safety evaluations.
- **Google DeepMind Blog**: Gemini releases, architecture technical reports, foundation model benchmarks.
- **Meta AI Blog & GitHub**: Llama open-weight releases, PyTorch core updates, vLLM optimization kernels.
- **Hugging Face Hub & Research**: Trending models, open leaderboard benchmarks, community fine-tuning evaluations.

## 2. Compute, Semiconductors & Physical Supply Chain
- **SemiAnalysis (Dylan Patel)**: In-depth semiconductor packaging, optical interconnects, AI cluster power efficiency, and wafer manufacturing economics.
- **AnandTech / Tom's Hardware / ServeTheHome**: Server hardware architecture teardowns, datacenter power delivery, and silicon bench tests.
- **TrendForce / Digitimes / Yole Group**: Foundry capacity utilization, memory contract pricing, advanced packaging supply allocation.
- **SEC Filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K)**: Primary disclosures for NVDA, TSM, AMD, AVGO, ASML, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, ORCL.

## 3. Open Source Ecosystem & Institutional Deep Dives
- **Hacker News (Y Combinator)**: Engineering practitioner discussions, real-world deployment friction, and latency trade-offs.
- **The Information**: Silicon Valley corporate reporting, enterprise software contract terms, and hyperscaler private financials.
- **Stratechery (Ben Thompson)**: Value chain aggregation theory, platform economics, and strategic positioning.
- **ArXiv (cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG)**: Fundamental algorithmic breakthroughs, transformer architecture refinements, and preprint papers.
