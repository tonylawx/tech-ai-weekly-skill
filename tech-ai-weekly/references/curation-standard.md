# Tech & AI Weekly: Curation Standard

Fact-checking, topic selection, and narrative structuring standards rooted in industrial content engineering and balance-sheet level industry research.

---

## 1. The Event-Led Framework: One Event, Full-Factor Penetration

Every topic section (`## 0`, `## 1`, `## 2`) must adhere to a strict five-tier progression:

1. **Tier 1: Factual Event Summary (One Event per Section)**
   - Open the section with objective, neutral journalism: date, principal corporate entities, action taken, and explicit figures (contract value, power capacity, chip counts, valuation delta).
   - Never inject subjective emotion, hyperbolic buzzwords, or premature editorializing in the opening paragraph.

2. **Tier 2: Penetrating PR to Audit the Micro-Ledger**
   - Strip away corporate marketing slogans ("breakthrough architecture," "ecosystem empowerment," "multi-billion commitment"). Reconstruct the underlying financial and operational balance sheet.
   - Audit three balance-sheet dimensions:
     - **Unit Economics**: Inference cost per token, gross margin per API call, and subscription customer acquisition cost (CAC).
     - **Physical Capital Overhead**: Grid connection tariffs (cents/kWh), substation construction schedules, cooling water depletion, and liquid cooling CDU retrofits.
     - **Balance Sheet Drag**: Annualized depreciation expense generated by multi-billion CapEx cycles and who carries the asset write-down risk.

3. **Tier 3: Structural Conflicts & Incentive Alignment**
   - Dissect deep value-chain frictions: high gross margins of upstream foundries and chip designers vs. margin compression among downstream enterprise software providers; open-source model parity vs. proprietary defensive price cuts; stochastic model outputs vs. zero-fault-tolerance production environments.
   - Strictly avoid moralizing or conspiracy framing; examine objective Nash equilibria under real-world constraints.

4. **Tier 4: Historical Mirroring & Temporal Depth**
   - Map current developments against historical isomorphic precedents (e.g., the 1999 telecom dark fiber bust, early 2000s Linux disruption of proprietary Unix, or early AWS cloud price wars).
   - Compare underlying mechanisms to isolate invariant economic laws from transient hype.

5. **Tier 5: Verifiable Forward Deduction & Falsification Criteria**
   - Provide a concrete, directional deduction with an explicit time horizon (e.g., next 2–3 quarters).
   - Establish transparent falsification variables: specify exact quarterly metrics (e.g., hyperscaler operating margins, transformer lead times, chip order revisions) that would validate or disprove the thesis.

## 2. Cognitive Ladder & The Two-Turn Rule

1. **70% Familiarity + 30% Hardcore Institutional Increment**
   - **70% Familiarity On-Ramp**: Anchor the beginning with high-resonance industry news already familiar to the broader market (valuation repricing, canceled data center permits, mega-cap earnings).
   - **30% Hardcore Increment**: Escalate into specialized engineering and financial details rarely covered by mainstream media (SEC footnote adjustments, high-voltage transformer lead times, PJM interconnection queues, CoWoS packaging yield curves).

2. **Strict Two-Turn Logic Hook**
   - Avoid flat descriptive summaries (0 turns) or emotionally reactive single reversals (1 turn).
   - Maintain the golden two-turn sequence:
     - Consensus Expectation A $\to$
     - Micro-Ledger & Operational Reversal B $\to$
     - Systemic Institutional & Physical Bottleneck C.

---

## 3. Evidence Standards & Fact-Checking

1. **Rule of Independent Corroboration (No Single-Source Claims)**
   - Critical performance benchmarks, valuation deltas, or supply chain order adjustments must be corroborated by at least **two independent sources** (e.g., official regulatory filings + authoritative wire reporting, or two independent equity research supply-chain checks).
   - Unverified social media rumors or single-blogger leaks must be explicitly labeled as "unconfirmed industry rumors."

2. **Precise Timeline & Version Provenance**
   - Anchor technical milestones to exact publication or patent timestamps. Never mistake repackaged research papers from previous years for novel industry breakthroughs.

3. **Neutral Engineering & Institutional Tone**
   - Strictly prohibit exclamations and sensationalism ("game-changing," "epic destruction," "guaranteed windfall").
   - Employ precise engineering and financial terminology (e.g., "context degradation rate," "memory bandwidth saturation," "annual recurring revenue (ARR) retention").

---

## 4. The Binder Protocol (Accountability & Historical Consistency)

1. **Public Review of Prior Theses**
   - Systematically cross-check earlier industry deductions against realized corporate results and regulatory filings.
   - When a forecast deviates, explicitly diagnose which parameter drifted (e.g., underestimated open-source quantization efficiency or overestimated utility interconnect approval speed).

2. **Long-Term Structural Consistency**
   - Maintain coherence across analytical frameworks over time, resisting short-term speculative market narratives.
