# Tech & AI Weekly (科技与 AI 周刊)

Use this skill to produce Sunday's **Tech & AI Weekly Digest (科技与 AI 周刊)**. 
This skill applies **Industrial Media Methodology** and **Industrial Content Engineering Standards** to transform fragmented weekly tech news, vendor hype, and PR spin into institutional-grade industrial research featuring rigorous world models, balance-sheet ledger accounting, and deep conflict mapping.

---

## Core Mission: Constructing World Models, Rejecting PR Spin & Emotional Clickbait

The primary mission of an analyst is not trafficking in emotional clickbait or regurgitating corporate press releases, but **helping readers construct clear cognitive world models that drastically lower the marginal cost of communication and decision-making**.
Every weekly digest issue is an industrial-grade progression: **Micro-Level Facts $\to$ Real Capital Ledger $\to$ Value-Chain Conflict $\to$ Historical Mirroring $\to$ Falsifiable Deduction**.

### Core Methodological Pillars

1. **Follow the Capital Ledger & Real Costs**:
   - Every technological advance and commercial maneuver carries benefits, but **unavoidable real-world costs**.
   - Strip away PR rhetoric ("breakthrough compute," "thriving ecosystem") to audit three operational dimensions:
     - **Micro Unit Economics**: Token inference cost deltas vs. API gross margins;
     - **Full-Factor Physical Overhead**: Industrial power tariffs (cents/kWh), substation interconnection queues, datacenter cooling water loss, and liquid cooling CDU retrofits;
     - **Balance Sheet & Depreciation Drag**: Who absorbs the hundreds of billions in annual depreciation generated by massive CapEx cycles?
2. **Structure of Conflicts over Moralizing**:
   - Reject moralistic or conspiratorial framing. Layoffs, canceled site permits, price wars, and export restrictions represent the objective Nash equilibrium of diverse economic actors operating under hard constraints.
   - Map four fundamental tensions: **Upstream picks-and-shovels providers (NVIDIA/TSMC) high gross margins vs. downstream model/software margin bleeding; proprietary price-defense cuts vs. open-source technical parity; energy-hungry datacenters vs. local utility grid safety; equity market cash-flow payback expectations vs. research lab scaling-law purity**.
3. **The Cognitive Ladder (70% Familiarity On-Ramp + 30% Hardcore Institutional Increment)**:
   - **70% Familiarity**: Anchor the section in widely recognized, resonant headlines (e.g., valuation repricing, canceled datacenter projects, or nuclear power restarts);
   - **30% Hardcore Increment**: Deliver specialized industrial depth unavailable in generic media (e.g., SEC footnote disclosures, PJM interconnect queue statistics, CoWoS packaging yield bottlenecks, high-voltage transformer lead times).
4. **The Two-Turn Cognitive Hook**:
   - Avoid flat descriptive summaries (0 turns) or emotionally reactive single reversals (1 turn). Maintain the golden two-turn sequence:
     - **Consensus Expectation A**: The headline narrative and surface-level reaction;
     - **First Turn (Reality B)**: Penetrate the micro-ledger to expose structural flaws and cash-flow burns beneath apparent prosperity;
     - **Second Turn (Systemic Truth C)**: Reveal underlying institutional constraints and physical bottlenecks that force actors into this dilemma, establishing an enduring world model for the reader.
5. **Historical Mirroring & Temporal Depth**:
   - Frame current AI trends within modern industrial history (e.g., **1999 North American Telecom Dark Fiber Mania**, **early 2000s Linux Displacement of Commercial Unix**, **2010s Early Cloud Infrastructure Price Wars**).
6. **The Half-Tree Rule & Road Transition Curves**:
   - Follow the "half-tree" model: either "causal trunk $\to$ parallel branches" or "parallel observations $\to$ causal synthesis." Never oscillate erratically between frameworks.
   - Smooth transitions between sections with gradual spiral curves, connecting paragraphs like a well-engineered highway.
7. **The Binder Protocol (Falsifiable Hypotheses)**:
   - Provide 1–2 definitive 2–3 quarter deductions with explicit, testable falsification criteria.

## Volume & Richness Mandate

To prevent superficial or skeletal summaries, every digest draft must adhere to strict volume and paragraph density requirements:

1. **Total Character Count**:
   - **Main body text (excluding chart JSON fences) must achieve 3200–4800 Chinese characters** (standard deep feature article). Drafts below 2800 characters are strictly rejected.
2. **Paragraph Density & Progression Sequence**:
   - Every `##` section must contain **4–6 substantial paragraphs** (each 150–280 Chinese characters). Two-paragraph superficial summaries are prohibited.
   - Every topic section must advance through the five-tier progression:
     - **Paragraph 1 (Factual Evidence)**: Detail date, entities, transaction figures, and direct consequences without emotional editorializing;
     - **Paragraph 2 (Micro-Ledger Audit)**: Dissect unit economics, power costs, depreciation schedules, and cash flow impacts;
     - **Paragraph 3 (Value-Chain Conflicts)**: Analyze structural friction between upstream and downstream participants;
     - **Paragraph 4 (Historical Mirror & Physical Ceiling)**: Contrast against historical analogs, highlighting physical (grid/packaging) or institutional bottlenecks;
     - **Paragraph 5 (Forward Deduction & Falsification)**: State clear directional deductions and testable variables.

## Title & Structural Hard Rules

1. **Main Title (H1)**:
   - **Branded Column Prefix**: Must begin with `【科技周报】` (or `【科技与AI周报】`).
   - **Single Lead Event Focus**: Do not string multiple disconnected events together with commas. Select the single most impactful, disruptive event of the week as the primary spine.
   - **Compelling & Conflict-Driven**: Highlight sharp contrast, valuation reality, or physical bottlenecks. Avoid flat bureaucratic announcements.
   - **Strictly No Colons**: Never use colons (neither `:` nor `：`). Avoid `A: B` templates; construct fluid, engaging narrative titles.
   - Conclude with target date in `YYYYMMDD` format.
2. **Subheadings (H2)**:
   - `## 0`, `## 1`, `## 2`, `## 3` must use natural narrative phrases. Strictly no colons (`:` or `：`).
3. **Public Prose Cleanliness**:
   - The final section (`## 3`) must provide natural industry outlook and forward deduction. Never leak internal pipeline jargon like "合订本推演：" or "后续由事实合订本核对检验".

- **`## 0 [Lead Event Narrative Title, No Colons]`**:
  - *Primary Headline Event (e.g., mega-cap valuation repricing, major architecture shift)*
  - 4–5 substantial paragraphs: Ground truth facts $\to$ Capital ledger $\to$ Value-chain friction $\to$ Historical mirror $\to$ Falsifiable outlook.
- **`## 1 [Infrastructure & Hardware Title, No Colons]`**:
  - *Secondary Event (e.g., compute infrastructure, power grid, packaging yield)*
  - 4–5 substantial paragraphs with a data chart (```chart JSON fence).
  - Bold relevant equity tickers (e.g., `**NVDA**`, `**TSM**`, `**AMZN**`, `**MSFT**`, `**GOOGL**`).
- **`## 2 [Enterprise Adoption & Software Title, No Colons]`**:
  - *Tertiary Event (e.g., enterprise deployment friction, open-source competition, commercialization)*
  - 4–5 substantial paragraphs detailing hidden integration costs, hallucination mitigation expenses, and revenue durability.
- **`## 3 [Industry Outlook & Strategic Deduction, No Colons]`**:
  - Synthesize 1–2 irreversible medium-to-long term industry trends with clear falsification horizons.
  - Distill core takeaway points and an engaging question suitable for X publication.

### ❌ Superficial Negative Example (Sparse word count, no micro-ledger, zero turns)
> 亚马逊与弗吉尼亚劳登县达成协议，退回了 122 英亩数据中心用地。AI 建设面临电网容量不足的问题。现在变电站扩容审批要好几年，算力建设和公用电网产生了矛盾。未来投资应该关注能源股。

### ✅ Industrial Deep Paragraph Standard (Rich texture, micro-ledger accounting, structural conflict, historical mirror, two turns)
> 亚马逊 **AMZN** 与弗吉尼亚州劳登县正式签署土地协议，撤回原定在乔治华盛顿大学旧校区占地 122 英亩的数据中心建设项目。根据协议披露文件，这片原计划容纳数十万台 AI 算力服务器的核心地块被整体移交用于住宅开发与社区混合用地，亚马逊不仅需退回前期平整的土地，还需向县住房基金额外注资 500 万美元作为补偿。
>
> 撕开企业社会责任公关通稿，底层是一笔极其残酷的微观财务账。劳登县所在的 PJM 电网互联排队名单上，等待并网审批的电力申请已经排到了 2030 年以后。一个标准 100 兆瓦（MW）的大型 AI 算力集群，单月仅基础电费开销就超过 400 万美元，而若要单独向电网申请建设一条 500 千伏专用高压输电线路及配套变电站，前期沉没资本开支就高达 1.2 亿至 1.8 亿美元，且工期至少需要 48 个月。对于按季度背负数百亿美元 CapEx 考核的云厂商而言，将数十亿美元的高性能算力卡囤在仓库里等待通电，每推迟一个季度就会产生数亿美元的固定折旧损失。退地止损，是资产负债表在电网硬约束面前被迫做出的财务止血。
>
> 这背后暴露出的是深刻的区域利益冲突。对于劳登县当地居民而言，过去五年数据中心虽贡献了全县超过三成的房产税收入，但庞大的高密度机柜噪音、巨大的变压器冷却耗水，以及随之而来的居民阶梯电价上涨，已经引发了选民的强烈反弹。地方监管机构在“科技税收”与“选民选票”之间迅速倒向后者，将并网门槛和环保容积率收紧到近乎惩罚性的水平。地方公用事业委员会不再无条件为科技巨头的绿电承诺买单。
>
> 这种基础设施的错配，让人不可避免地联想起 1999 年北美电信光纤泡沫。彼时世通（WorldCom）等电信巨头在全美铺设了数百万公里的长途骨干光纤，最终因最后一公里的接入网络建设严重滞后，导致全美超过 90% 的光纤变成了暗光纤（Dark Fiber），引发了通信行业的全面资产减记。如今，AI 算力产业链正面临着几乎完全同构的物理死锁：算力服务器的交付速度以周为单位，而重型电力变压器、特高压开关设备和核电基荷机组的交付周期却以年为单位。
>
> 这一深层结构性矛盾，直接重构了二级市场的投资逻辑。单纯拥有算法参数的大模型企业，在未来两到三个季度将面临折旧与电费的双重绞杀；而那些真正掌握专有基荷能源接入权、掌控关键液冷配电工程交付能力的重工业企业，才构成了算力扩张深水区中最坚固的护城河。
